## 🎯 Research Focus

- **Epidemiological surveillance and public health**
  - Dengue surveillance and decision support systems in Brazil
  - News monitoring and alerting for public health intelligence
  - Causal inference with difference-in-differences for vaccination policy evaluation
- **Multicriteria decision-making**
  - Value-Focused Thinking (VFT) and FITradeoff
  - Outranking methods (PROMETHEE-ROC)
  - Prioritization of vector control actions
- **Operations research and optimization**
  - Mathematical programming (LP, MILP, NLP)
  - Simulation and prescriptive analytics
- **Applied statistics and machine learning**
  - Supervised and unsupervised learning
  - Time-series forecasting and regression modeling
